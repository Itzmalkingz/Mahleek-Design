About Mahleek

I design from the place where business goals meet emotion.

I am a Lagos-based brand designer and web developer building identities, websites, and campaign visuals for businesses that want to be taken seriously. My process is simple: understand what makes the brand valuable, remove visual confusion, then create an experience that feels clear, premium, and easy to trust.

I care about the details clients notice and the details they only feel: spacing, hierarchy, tone, speed, mobile polish, image direction, and the confidence a brand gives before anyone reads a word.

Design philosophy

Every screen should make the business feel more valuable, more trustworthy, and easier to choose.

01
BioWellness - Wellness Brand

A calm, credible wellness website designed to make care feel professional and easy to begin.

BioWellness needed a digital presence that felt peaceful without looking generic. I shaped the layout, tone, and visual rhythm around clarity, trust, and fast decision-making.

Problem
The offer needed more clarity and a stronger first impression.
Strategy
Soft visual hierarchy, benefit-led sections, and low-friction calls to action.
Result
A cleaner customer journey that makes the brand feel more established.

02
Prestige Barber - Local Service

A sharp booking-focused website that gives a barbershop the confidence of a premium brand.

The goal was to move beyond a basic online page and create a visual experience that feels stylish, direct, and built for clients ready to book.

Problem
The brand needed a stronger digital image to match the in-shop experience.
Strategy
Bold contrast, direct service framing, and prominent conversion paths.
Result
A more premium service perception with clearer reasons to take action.

03
Tutorial Haven - Education Platform

A structured learning website built to make discovery simple and content feel dependable.

Tutorial Haven needed a website that could hold information without overwhelming students. I focused on organization, readable sections, and a calm browsing flow.

Problem
Educational content needed better structure and easier navigation.
Strategy
Clean content grouping, predictable hierarchy, and responsive layouts.
Result
A more usable platform that helps students find direction quickly.
